Connect your phone to Clavra

This page shows your Mac's terminal tabs on your phone. It opens on its own when you scan the code from Clavra.

  1. On your Mac, open Clavra and choose Phone Access in the sidebar, then Turn On.
  2. Point your phone's camera at the QR code. This page opens with your Mac's key.
  3. Click Allow on the Mac. Your tabs appear here; pick one and type.

The key in the code never leaves your phone and Mac, so nobody in between, this site included, can read what you type or see.
